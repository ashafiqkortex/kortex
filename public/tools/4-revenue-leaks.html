The 4 Revenue Leaks

Every home services company loses money to these four operational gaps. AI can plug them all.

$150K - $500K
Typical annual revenue leak for a $3M-$10M home services company
1

Missed Calls

27% of calls go unanswered

85% of unanswered calls don't leave voicemail. 67% call your competitor instead. 42% of calls come after hours when you're closed.

AI Fix: 24/7 AI Phone Agent answers every call, qualifies leads, books jobs directly into your system
2

Unsold Estimates

50% of estimates die without follow-up

Average contractor follows up 1.2 times. Best practice is 5 touches. Systematic follow-up improves close rates by 8-12 percentage points.

AI Fix: Automated follow-up at Day 1, 3, 7 via text/email. Tracks which estimates need attention.
3

Dormant Customers

60% of your database is dormant

Past customers who haven't used you in 12+ months. Reactivating them costs 3-5x less than new customer acquisition. They spend 31% more.

AI Fix: Personalized reactivation campaigns, maintenance reminders, referral automation
4

Reputation Gap

Each 1-star gap = 5-9% revenue loss

4.5-star businesses get 3x more clicks than 4.0-star. 37% of negative reviews cite communication problems — exactly what AI solves.

AI Fix: Automated review collection, AI responses to all reviews, competitor monitoring
