How We Calculate Revenue Leaks

  • Missed Calls: 27% of home services calls go unanswered (industry average). 46% of answered calls convert to jobs. We calculate lost revenue from the gap.
  • Unsold Estimates: 50% of estimates go unsold without follow-up. Systematic 5-touch follow-up recovers 8-12% of those lost deals.
  • Dormant Customers: 60% of past customers are dormant (no service in 12+ months). 10% can be reactivated with targeted outreach. Existing customers spend 31% more than new ones.
  • Reputation Gap: Each star rating difference impacts revenue by 5-9%. Businesses at 4.5+ stars get 3x more clicks than those at 4.0 stars.
