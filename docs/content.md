## Brand thesis: The Truth Layer

Every business runs on two layers. The top layer is the tools — CRMs, dashboards, tickets, reports. The layer underneath is reality: what customers are actually doing, what technicians are actually doing, what the AI is actually producing, what the business is actually worth right now. Most companies optimize the top layer. We build the truth layer — the systems that operate on what's really happening, not what's been entered.

**Tagline candidates:**
1. **"We build the truth layer under your operations."** ← my pick (thesis-forward, SEO-rich)
2. "Systems that operate on reality, not reports."
3. "Ground truth, engineered."

## SERVICES PAGE (deep dives)

### Workflow Automation

**Headline:** Operational systems that replace the chaos between tools.

Most "automation" is glue — if-this-then-that rules bolted between SaaS products. It works until it doesn't, and when it breaks, nobody knows where. We build real operational systems: custom backends that orchestrate work end-to-end, with observability, error handling, and the kind of stability that lets you stop thinking about them.

**We build:**
- Custom ticketing and dispatch systems
- End-to-end billing and invoicing pipelines
- Geo-aware dispatch and routing
- Live pulse dashboards
- Queue-based job orchestration
- Multi-system state machines

---

### Custom Agents

**Headline:** AI agents that do work, not just answer questions.

The interesting problem with agents isn't "can they think" — it's "can they act safely inside your business." We build domain-trained agents with real tool access, persistent memory, and the governance layer to keep them from doing the wrong thing at scale. Sales agents, research agents, ops agents, support agents. Deployed into your stack, not chatted with on a demo site.

**We build:**
- Tool-using agents with production guardrails
- Multi-agent coordination systems
- Long-running autonomous workflows
- Human-in-the-loop escalation patterns
- Agent evaluation and observability

---

### AI Engineering

**Headline:** Production-grade LLM systems that survive real users.

Prototyping an LLM feature takes a weekend. Running it in production — with evals, latency budgets, cost controls, hallucination guards, and a path to iterate — takes engineering. We design and build the full stack: retrieval, generation, evaluation, deployment.

**We build:**
- RAG systems with real retrieval quality
- Evaluation and regression-test pipelines
- Fine-tuning and distillation workflows
- Prompt caching and cost optimization
- Multi-model routing and fallback

---

### AI Governance

**Headline:** A living nervous system, not a policy document.

AI governance fails when it's static — a PDF that says "we do not allow bias" sitting in a Notion wiki. Real governance is continuous: it watches your codebase, your prompts, your outputs, your customer trust signals, and surfaces drift the moment it appears. When your business logic changes, governance changes. That's what we call the Truth Layer.

**We build:**
- Continuous governance monitoring
- Drift, leak, and duplication detection
- Customer-trust fracture surfacing
- Regional and regulatory compliance mapping
- Agent and model review systems

---

### Integrations

**Headline:** When your tools don't talk, we rewire them.

Every mid-sized company has the same silent problem: a dozen systems that each hold a piece of the truth and none of which agree. QuickBooks says one thing, the CRM says another, the field app says a third. We build the integration layer that reconciles them — bidirectional, real-time, resilient to schema drift.

**We build:**
- QuickBooks, Salesforce, HubSpot, DocuSign integrations
- Custom API orchestration
- Physical-to-digital (IoT, telemetry, field hardware)
- Webhooks, event buses, streaming pipelines
- Reconciliation and de-duplication layers

### Case Study 2: Truth Layer — Living AI Governance

**Client profile:** AI-native product company with multi-surface customer exposure. [CONFIRM industry if shareable — e.g., fintech, healthtech.]

**The problem.** AI governance was a compliance exercise — a PDF of principles disconnected from the code. Meanwhile, real issues were compounding silently: prompt drift across agents, data bleeds between contexts, duplicated logic producing inconsistent outputs, customer trust fractures invisible until churn. Traditional governance tools couldn't see any of it, because they were never wired into the product in the first place.

**What we built.**
- A continuous ingestion layer: consumes codebase, prompts, business philosophy, and regional compliance rules
- Drift detection: flags when agent behavior diverges from stated intent
- Gap and duplication scanner: identifies where logic is missing or contradictorily implemented in multiple places
- Data leak surfacing: finds PII and context bleeds across agent boundaries
- Multi-surface trust mapping: identifies the exact customer touchpoints where trust begins eroding
- Automated agent review: continuous critique of agent behavior against governance charter
- Self-updating: when code or business logic changes, Truth Layer regenerates its understanding automatically

**Outcome.** Governance moved from a quarterly review meeting to a live system inside the product. [CONFIRM: # drift events caught pre-production, compliance audit outcomes, customer trust metric shifts.]

**The thesis in action.** Most governance tools describe what an AI system should do. Truth Layer observes what it's actually doing — and surfaces the gap.

### Case Study 3: Sales Intelligence — Behavioral Funnel Truth

**Client profile:** B2B SaaS sales organization. [CONFIRM segment/ACV if shareable.]

## ABOUT

**H1:** We are an engineering firm disguised as a consultancy.

Kortex was built on a simple conviction: most of what's sold as "AI consulting" is deck production and tool recommendation. The companies that actually get value from AI and automation are the ones that partner with engineers — people who sit with the problem, design the system, write the code, deploy it, and stay long enough to see it change the business.

We build the truth layer under our clients' operations. That means we're less interested in the tool landscape than in what the tools are missing. Less interested in dashboards than in what happens before things make it to a dashboard. We care about ground truth — the state of the business as it actually is, not as it's been reported.

We work across five disciplines — workflow automation, custom agents, AI engineering, AI governance, and integrations — because real operational problems don't respect service categories. A broken sales funnel is also a data problem, an agent problem, and an integration problem. We engineer across the whole surface.

### Principles

**1. Ship, don't strategize.** First working prototype in week one. Always.

**2. Build to the metal.** Real code, not no-code approximations. Your systems are yours to own, not rented.

**3. Measure truth.** Every system we ship reports against external reality, not its own logs.

**4. Stay resident.** We don't vanish at launch. Governance, observability, and evolution are part of the engagement.

**5. Refuse the hype.** We'll tell you which parts of your problem don't need AI. Usually most of them.
